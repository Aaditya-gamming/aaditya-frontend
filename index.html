About Me

I am currently a Class 11 student at Kathmandu Model Secondary School, where I am diving deep into Physical Science and Computer Science under the NEB curriculum. While I enjoy the logic and structure of my core science subjects, my real drive comes from applying that knowledge to create things from scratch.

💻 What I Do

I am incredibly passionate about technology and digital creation. When I am not focused on my coursework, you can usually find me working on a new project. My core interests include:

  • Coding & Web Development: I love writing code and designing functional, clean websites.
  • Game Development: Playing games is great, but building my own video games from the ground up is even better. I enjoy the entire process of bringing digital worlds to life.
  • Teaching Others: I genuinely enjoy breaking down concepts and helping other people learn. If I figure out how to do something cool, my first instinct is to share it.

  • Valorant
  • Hollow Knight
  • Call of Duty: Mobile
  • Free Fire

Whether I am debugging a website, climbing the ranks in a shooter, or helping a classmate understand a difficult topic, I am always looking for the next challenge.
